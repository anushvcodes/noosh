⬇ Download for Mac Try it in your browser →

Mac app is universal (Apple Silicon + Intel). The browser version needs no install and works on Windows too.

  • Open the downloaded NOOSH.dmg and drag NOOSH into your Applications folder.
  • Open NOOSH. The first time, right-click it → Open → Open (macOS asks this because the app is shared directly, not through the App Store).
  • Allow Microphone, Speech Recognition and Accessibility when asked.
  • Look for the small N in your menu bar. Hold ⌥, say something, and let go.
There's no native app for your system yet — so use the browser version above. It does voice-to-text with a Copy button, no install needed.

Privacy & Legal

NOOSH — voice to text, hands free.

🔒 Zero Data Guarantee

NOOSH does not record, store, or harvest any audio files, voice commands, or personal transcripts on external servers. Voice recognition is processed ephemerally and never retained.

📊 Google Analytics

Anonymous site traffic, visitor counts, and session durations are measured with Google Analytics solely to improve app performance. This data never includes audio, voice commands, or transcripts.

🔗 Third-Party Apps

NOOSH independently routes your commands to platforms such as WhatsApp, Google, and Gemini. It is not officially affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any of these platforms.
