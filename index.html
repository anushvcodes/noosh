NOOSH

Hold ⌥ Option anywhere and talk — your words appear as text.

The eyes follow your cursor — move your mouse 👀

Built by someone who believes in efficiency — and is far too lazy to type. 😌

⬇ Download for Mac Try it in your browser →

Mac app is universal (Apple Silicon + Intel). The browser version needs no install and works on Windows too.

  • Open the downloaded NOOSH.dmg and drag NOOSH into your Applications folder.
  • Open NOOSH. The first time, right-click it → Open → Open (macOS asks this because the app is shared directly, not through the App Store).
  • Allow Microphone, Speech Recognition and Accessibility when asked.
  • Look for the small N in your menu bar. Hold ⌥, say something, and let go.
Only macOS can run the full app (it types into any app for you). On Windows, use the “Try it in your browser” button — it does voice-to-text with a Copy button.

Everything is transcribed on your device — nothing is uploaded.
